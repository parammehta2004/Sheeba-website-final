## 1. BRAND ARCHETYPE

### Primary: **The Sage + The Healer**

Sheeba occupies a rare dual archetype that is consistent across every page:

**The Sage** — She is the world's foremost authority in her niche. The proof:
- 10 distinct credentials across USA, functional medicine, herbology, quantum healing
- Consulting nutritionist for Men's Health Magazine Singapore
- Published author (*Edible to Incredible*)
- Speaker at Singapore General Hospital, Mount Elizabeth, DSTA, Republic Polytechnic
- 5 international awards, recommended by medical specialists across disciplines

The Sage's core message throughout the site: *"I see what doctors miss. I connect the dots."*
Evidence — verbatim from copy: *"Her biggest asset is connecting the dots and reading between the lines from blood tests and other assessments which are very often overlooked by healthcare practitioners."*

**The Healer** — She is not clinical. She is warm, personal, spiritual. The proof:
- Page load copy: *"Preparing our nutrition haven for you..."* and *"Opening our doors to you 🥰"*
- Form label: *"We're here to listen"*
- Testimonial label: *"Sweet words by our sweetest clients"*
- She goes to clients who cannot come to her
- She integrates body, mind, and spirit as a unified system
- She learned Biodynamic Craniosacral Therapy through her own personal healing journey

The Healer's core message: *"I will not treat your disease. I will restore you."*

### Secondary: **The Magician**

She promises transformation that conventional medicine declared impossible:
- *"I was told by my medical doctors that nothing could be done about my fatty liver... My liver enzymes were within normal range!"* (Joy C)
- *"In September this year, we found out I was 4 weeks pregnant!! Naturally, without fertility drugs!"* (Rachel)
- *"What doctors could not help me with, was solved in one session with Sheeba."* (Iris)
- *"After 27 doctors appointments, numerous tests and scans..."* — answered by one appointment with Sheeba (Laura)

The Magician archetype says: *"What you were told was impossible, I will make possible."*

### Archetype Hierarchy
```
PRIMARY:   Sage (authority, knowledge, research)
PRIMARY:   Healer (warmth, empathy, personal touch)  
SECONDARY: Magician (transformation, the impossible made real)
ABSENT:    Jester, Ruler, Outlaw — no humor, no hierarchy, no rebellion
```

## 2. EMOTIONAL POSITIONING

### The Client's Emotional State at Entry
The 20 testimonials reveal a consistent emotional journey *before* Sheeba. Clients arrive in one of three states:

**State A — Exhaustion after the medical system has failed them**
> *"After 27 doctors appointments, numerous tests and scans and a spiralling list of diagnoses, none of which seemed related, I felt overwhelmed."* — Laura, 25
> *"Nothing worked for me... I had just about given up."* — Gayathri, 27
> *"I visited Sheeba in the hope of finding a solution... as our last hope."* — Neha G (for her son)

**State B — Shame and loss of control over their own body**
> *"Managing my weight has become a daily struggle."* — Valerie, 42
> *"For 30 years, I was told... 'you should lose some weight'... not one of them gave me a precise road map."* — Andre, 37
> *"I had just about given up and committed myself to live a life of pain and humiliation."* — Gayathri, 27

**State C — Fear of medication dependence or chronic decline**
> *"My doctor threatened to put me on medications unless I showed some improvement."* — Rena, 43
> *"I had to consume medications every day for the last six years."* — Karina, 32
> *"I was diagnosed with type 2 diabetes Nov 2017 and had to start medication."* — Kenneth, 34

### Sheeba's Emotional Promise
The brand does not promise weight loss or blood test numbers. It promises **restoration of self**:
- *"I feel twenty years younger"* — Ruby, 71
- *"I finally feel like I'm living"* — Laura, 25
- *"I feel in control again"* — Valerie, 42
- *"It has not only helped me physically but has also boosted my self-confidence"* — Nicole, 44
- *"Sheeba has given me a new sense of life"* — Laura, 25

### The Emotional Arc the Brand Sells
```
ENTRY:    Exhausted → Failed → Resigned → Last Resort
TURNING:  "I connected with Sheeba" (the moment of hope)
EXIT:     Restored → Energised → Confident → Grateful → Evangelical
```

Clients don't just leave healthier — they become **brand advocates who proactively recommend her** ("I insisted on writing a testimonial", "I will continue to recommend you to all I know", "strongly recommend Sheeba to everyone"). This is the hallmark of a deeply emotional, trust-based brand.

### The Trust Mechanism
Sheeba does not sell on features. She sells on **a bridge between conventional medicine and something better**:
- She is *recommended by the same doctors who couldn't fix you*
- She reads the same blood tests your GP dismissed
- She is not alternative — she is *complementary and superior*

Positioning statement hidden in the copy:  
> *"This makes patients and doctors comfortable in recommending her or working with her to improve client's medical blood tests."*

She is simultaneously credible to skeptics (5 international awards, MSc in Human Nutrition, medical doctor referrals) and transformative to believers (Reconnective Healing, quantum physics, energetic medicine). She holds both worlds without contradiction.

## 4. VISUAL PERSONALITY

> *This section defines personality expressed through imagery, symbolism, texture, and motion — not interface elements.*

### What the existing site signals visually (derived from content, not redesign)

**Nature as intelligence, not decoration**  
The brand uses botanical imagery (Lottie plant animations, leaf motifs, food illustrations, avocado character) not as wellness clichés but as symbolic metaphors: plant stem cells (*Gemmotherapy*), nature-based healing that is *more intelligent than synthetic medicine*. Nature here means precision, not casualness.

**Warmth without softness**  
The brand is warm — *"Preparing our nutrition haven for you"*, *"Opening our doors to you 🥰"*, *"Sweet words by our sweetest clients"* — but it is not soft or vague. It is warm the way a brilliant doctor with exceptional bedside manner is warm: knowledgeable, certain, but human. The warmth comes from *confidence*, not from approachability for its own sake.

**The Practitioner's World**  
Imagery includes: Sheeba in a clinic setting (background video), Sheeba in green (her signature colour), food as medicine illustrations, blood chemistry charts, hair mineral diagrams. The visual world is simultaneously scientific and organic — test tubes next to plants.

**Transformation as a visible arc**  
The testimonial structure (entry state → outcome) creates a visual narrative of before/after — not cosmetically, but vitally. The imagery should echo this: things that were broken becoming whole. This is not a weight loss before/after brand. It is a *life* before/after brand.

### Brand Personality Attributes

| Attribute | Expression |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Tone of voice** | Warm authority — certain but never cold, personal but never casual |
| **Energy** | Still, grounded, unhurried — the opposite of medical urgency or wellness hustle |
| **Texture** | Organic, earned, layered — not polished or manufactured |
| **Movement** | Gentle, slow, purposeful — like the plant Lottie, like Biodynamic therapy itself |
| **Intimacy** | One-to-one — the brand always speaks to *you*, not to a crowd |
| **Trust signals** | Credentials → Awards → Referrals → Outcomes (in that order of proof) |

### The Brand Is NOT

- Not a clinical white-walls medical brand
- Not a glossy fitness/wellness influencer brand
- Not a spiritual/mystical brand (despite offering Reconnective Healing — it is always grounded in science first)
- Not a generic nutritionist brand (no generic food photography, no plate aesthetics)
- Not a corporate brand (personal name, personal story, personal touch)

### Brand Persona in One Sentence

> **Sheeba is the brilliant friend you never had — the one who happens to have a Master's in Human Nutrition, 10 certifications, 5 international awards, and the ability to read your blood test better than your doctor, but who you call not because she's impressive, but because she actually listens.**

## 6. BRAND TENSIONS (honest contradictions to be aware of)

These are real tensions within the brand that any future work must hold:

| Tension | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Science ↔ Spirituality** | She cites functional medicine blood chemistry *and* Reconnective Healing (quantum physics). Both are real to the brand. Must not collapse either direction. |
| **Award-winning ↔ Personal** | She is internationally recognised but her clients describe her as if she's their friend. Must not become corporate or distant. |
| **Accessible ↔ Premium** | She serves clients globally, goes to them if needed — but she is clearly a high-end practitioner. Must not feel mass-market. |
| **Ancient ↔ Modern** | 5,000-year-old essential oils *and* NES bio-field technology. Both coexist. The brand's superpower is that it does not choose. |
| **Individual ↔ Authoritative** | Every protocol is *yours*. But she has a body of expertise that cannot be challenged. Must not slide into arrogance or into relativism. |
